Before you continue
Allout is a neutral venue for lawful firearm transactions. To access firearm listings you must confirm the following.
I am at least 18 years of age and am legally permitted to purchase, possess, and sell firearms in my state and jurisdiction.
I agree to comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws regarding the purchase, sale, transfer, and possession of firearms.
I understand that Allout is a neutral listing venue only. Allout does not process payments, verify buyer eligibility, arrange transfers, or guarantee any listing or user.
Firearm transfers between residents of different states must be completed through a federally licensed dealer (FFL). Transactions involving prohibited persons, unlawful interstate transfers, or items restricted by state law are strictly prohibited.
